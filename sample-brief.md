# Sample Executive Morning Brief

This fictional example shows the type of structured morning brief the workflow was designed to produce.

## Emails Requiring Attention

### 1. Vendor Contract: Final Review Needed

**From:** Sarah Chen, General Counsel  
**Subject:** Vendor Contract: Final Review Needed  
**Priority:** High  
**Deadline:** Today at 3:00 PM

General Counsel has completed the revised vendor agreement and is waiting on approval of the updated limitation-of-liability language before implementation can begin next Monday.

**Suggested response:**

Thanks, Sarah. I'm comfortable moving forward with the revised language. Please proceed with finalizing the agreement and let me know if anything else requires my review before signature.

---

### 2. Final Candidate: Director of Operations

**From:** Elena Garcia, Recruiting Lead  
**Subject:** Final Candidate: Director of Operations  
**Priority:** Medium  
**Deadline:** Tomorrow at 10:00 AM

The candidate has completed all interviews and references. Elena is asking for a review of the final candidate notes before tomorrow's offer discussion.

**Recommended action:** Review candidate notes today and flag any concerns before the offer discussion.

---

### 3. Partnership Follow-Up

**From:** David Kim, Strategic Partner  
**Subject:** Partnership Follow-Up  
**Priority:** Low

David is following up on last week's conversation and asking whether you'd like to schedule a 30-minute call next week.

**Recommended action:** No immediate response required. This can be handled during scheduling review.

## Today's Meetings and Calls

- **8:30 AM: Daily Executive Check-In**
  - CEO + Executive Operations Partner

- **10:00 AM: Leadership Team Meeting**
  - Topics: operations performance, hiring priorities, and Q4 planning

- **1:00 PM: Vendor Negotiation**
  - CEO, CFO, General Counsel, Vendor Representatives
  - Relevant context: revised vendor contract language is awaiting final approval

- **3:30 PM: Product Strategy Review**
  - CEO + Product Leadership

- **5:00 PM: Investor Call**
  - CEO + CFO

## Tasks and Deadlines

- **Approve revised vendor contract language**
  - Due today by 3:00 PM

- **Confirm revised forecast assumptions with Finance**
  - Due by end of day

- **Review Director of Operations candidate notes**
  - Due before tomorrow's 10:00 AM offer discussion

- **Review two proposed headcount additions**
  - Department requests due Friday

## Items I Can Handle Without Executive Input

- Coordinate scheduling options for David Kim's follow-up call
- Follow up with Recruiting once candidate notes are reviewed
- Track the headcount request deadline and collect any missing information
- Confirm logistics and materials for today's leadership meeting

## Morning Meeting Focus

Recommended 10-minute discussion:

1. Approve or revise the vendor contract position
2. Confirm Finance forecast assumptions
3. Review any concerns with the Director of Operations candidate
4. Confirm headcount priorities
5. Flag anything from today's leadership or product meetings requiring advance preparation

## Notes

This example is fictional and created for portfolio purposes only.

The purpose of the workflow is not to replace executive judgment. It is designed to reduce the time spent gathering context and surface the few items most likely to require attention.
